# Bipolar Film Archive

This private repository will hold the research data behind the Bipolar Film Archive.

The CSV is currently a placeholder containing column headings only. No film records or evidence have been added yet.

## Data file

- `bipolar_film_archive.csv` - placeholder for archive records, including relevance labels, diagnosis subjects, evidence notes, and source links.

## Relevance labels

- **Central** - Bipolar disorder or lived experience is a main subject or major focus of the work.
- **Substantial** - It is an important part of a character's or participant's story, but not the work's main focus.
- **Incidental** - It appears as a brief or secondary reference, diagnosis, or discussion.

Each record will include evidence notes and source links to support its inclusion.
